

# My Experience with the EJPTv2

![1](</Writeups/Blogs/EJPTv2/img/certi.jpeg>)

## Introduction
A few months ago I successfully passed Elearn Security's EJPTv2, and in this post I'm going to tell you a bit about my experience, the knowledge I had before starting to prepare, how difficult I think the exam is, and the ways I consider best to fully prepare for it.

## My Experience
Personally it was a very fun exam; you rarely get the chance to have an environment like this one to practice and learn in.

Even so, because of my nerves I went through the exam very fast, and within the first 10 hours I had already completely finished it (you get a total of 2 days to do it). I think that was a mistake; if I did it again I would try to go slower and enjoy the exam.

## Background
In my case I had been studying hacking for a little over six months, studying 5-8 hours a day, and I really didn't do much preparation before the exam: it was one week of practicing with Metasploit (I was very rusty with it) and getting used to pivoting.

## Exam difficulty
This exam is very basic in difficulty. If you only have a tiny hacking foundation and you struggle with Easy-level machines, you're going to have a hard time, but if you study and practice day after day, within a few months you should be able to start preparing for this certification.

I also have to say that people are very afraid of the Pivoting in this exam; there's no need to worry about it, it's very basic pivoting which can be done with Metasploit without much trouble. In my case I used these videos to prepare for pivoting with Metasploit.

Pivoting on Linux –> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RotyKByc8Jc&t

Pivoting on windows –> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WM8lHCHblDU

As my own recommendation, the machines you'll come across in the exam are Easy-Medium level on TryHackMe or Easy on Hack The Box.

## How to prepare?
To prepare for this certification you have a wide variety of courses; the most interesting ones would be these:

- ElearnSecurity's own course: The course they offer is +150 and if you watch it all the way through, you go into the exam prepared, but in my opinion they explain things very slowly, and they only give you the bare minimum to pass the exam.

- The Hack4u academy: The academy's introduction to hacking course prepares you more than enough for this certification; if you understand all the content they cover, you could sit the EWPT. It's +50 hours of course taught by S4vitar.

- Studying without paying: You can prepare on your own, doing machines on platforms like HackTheBox or TryHackMe or watching videos from content creators like El Pingüino de Mario or IPPSEC.
